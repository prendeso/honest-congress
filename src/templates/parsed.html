📊 Parsed Documents

Parsed Documents are disclosures and PTRs that have been processed to extract structured data like assets, transactions, and liabilities.

Appearing here means the parser ran, not that it worked. The Read column says how much of each document it managed to extract, and names the filings that are scans of paper forms — those hold no machine-readable text at all, so their zeroes are the form’s doing rather than the parser’s.
