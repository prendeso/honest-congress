⏱️ Filing Compliance

The two least interpretive things this project computes. Neither makes any claim about intent, profit or conflict.

The STOCK Act requires a Periodic Transaction Report within 45 days of a covered trade. This is the subtraction of two dates that both appear on the filing, and nothing else. It is the one output here that does not depend on a threshold anybody chose.
